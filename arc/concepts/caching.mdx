ARC's decision context and a provider's generation context are different things. Reusing text in your application does not prove that either service served it from cache.

<span id="responses" />

## ARC models

The proposed launch rate card separates uncached input, cache reads, and output. The routing fee is waived for ARC models. A cache hit must be reported by the actual service; do not infer it solely from repeated prompts.

## Decisions

The launch rate card has input and cache-read categories only. Your inference provider meters its own input, caches, and output separately.

Today ARC reads every Decisions request in full, with or without `x-conversation-id`: each response reports all of it in `usage.encoded_input_tokens`, and `usage.cache_read_tokens` is 0. Re-using a conversation's already-read context, reported as `cache_read_tokens` and billed at the cache-read rate, is planned; this page will say when it applies.

ARC also weighs your provider's cache when it decides. Moving a conversation to a different model can discard that provider's cached prefix, so staying on the current model is often the cheaper choice. See [conversations](/arc/reference/decisions#conversations).

Generated output may become input on a later turn. Count each actual metered event once; do not add an extra copy merely because the same text has appeared before.


Use [usage and billing](/platform/usage) to distinguish estimates from charges, and [ARC pricing](/arc/pricing) for the launch token categories.
