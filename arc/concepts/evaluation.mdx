<Note>ARC is in private preview. Contact [hello@rayline.ai](mailto:hello@rayline.ai) to discuss access for your evaluation.</Note>

When live inference becomes available, compare the same representative tasks under a fixed baseline and an ARC checkpoint:

1. Define success before running the task: passing tests, correct answers, or a reviewed rubric.
2. Keep inputs, tools, timeouts, and retry limits comparable.
3. Record the checkpoint, selected model, input/cache/output tokens, end-to-end latency, and final task outcome.
4. Include failed attempts and retries in the cost per successful task.
5. Report the workload, sample size, and distribution of results alongside any aggregate savings.

For Decisions, add ARC usage to independently executed provider inference. For Responses, the endpoint's token rates include selection and generation; do not add a second Decisions fee.

A decision score is not a calibrated probability. Neither the pricing reference basket nor an estimated baseline saving is an evaluation of your workload.
