## Choose the right product

Use **ARC** when you want adaptive model selection. Use [Raydock](/raydock) to connect tools on your device to local models and cloud services. Choose [ARC Dynamic](/arc/dynamic) when you want to set routing value and temperature. Use [Workshop](/workshop) when you want to build directly in an app.
