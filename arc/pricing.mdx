<Rates />

- **ARC Swift and ARC Sage** bill every request at the tier's fixed card, whichever model serves it. See [Swift and Sage](/arc/models).
- **Configurable ARC (`arc-1.0`)** bills at the served model's Rayline catalog price, with no ARC fee. See [configure ARC routing](/arc/gateway-routing).
- **Decisions** bills the tokens ARC reads to decide, once per request, however many results it returns. Your provider bills the inference you run. Prices you send in `routing.prices` change which model ARC selects, not this fee. See [Decisions](/arc/decisions).

The rates above come from Rayline's effective price card; the card version and date are shown with them. Each request records the rate it was billed at. See [funding and billing](/platform/billing). [Agent Gateway](/rules-router/pricing) has a 0% commission policy, and Workshop has its own [plans and credits](/workshop/pricing/plans).
