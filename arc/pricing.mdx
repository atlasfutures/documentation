<Rates />

Responses includes routing and generation, with no additional Decisions charge for the same call.

Decisions is billed on encoded input and cache reads. Your chosen provider charges for inference separately. The fixed Decisions rates were calibrated to approximately 2.5% of a reference blended workload's inference cost; they are not a percentage commission or a guarantee across workloads.

For explicit coding-agent policies, [Agent Gateway](/rules-router/pricing) has a 0% commission launch policy. Workshop has its own [plans and credits](/workshop/pricing/plans).
