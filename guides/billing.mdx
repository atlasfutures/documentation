Rayline uses a prepaid balance model. You add funds, use them as you route requests, and top up when needed.

## Pricing model

**Pass-through + 7.5% routing fee.**

You pay the model provider's list price for tokens consumed, plus a 7.5% fee for Rayline's routing service. No platform fees, no monthly minimums, no subscriptions.

Example: If a request costs `$0.10` in tokens at provider list price, you pay `$0.1075` through Rayline.

<Tip>
The 7.5% fee is more than offset by routing savings. When Rayline routes a subagent call from Sonnet (`$15`/MTok output) to DeepSeek V4 Pro (`$3.48`/MTok output), the net savings are ~75% — even after the markup. Most users see 50–80% savings on subagent spend.
</Tip>

See [Model Pricing](/pricing/model-pricing) for per-model costs.

## Adding funds

### During onboarding

When you first sign up, the setup flow offers preset amounts:

- `$5`, `$10`, `$25`, `$50`, or a custom amount
- Payment via credit/debit card (Stripe)

### From the dashboard

Go to [platform.rayline.ai](https://platform.rayline.ai) and navigate to your account to add funds anytime.

## Auto top-up

Enable auto top-up to avoid running out of balance mid-session:

1. Go to **Setup** or **Account** in the dashboard
2. Toggle **Auto top-up** on
3. Set the **top-up amount** (how much to add each time)
4. Set the **threshold** (recharge when balance drops below this amount)

Example: Auto top-up `$10` when balance drops below `$5`.

<Warning>
If auto top-up is disabled and your balance hits zero, requests will fail. Enable auto top-up for uninterrupted routing.
</Warning>

## Checking your balance

- **Dashboard** — balance is shown on the Router page
- **Rayline app** — balance displayed in the Account section
- **CLI** — `rayline status` shows your current balance

## What counts as usage

Every request routed through Rayline is metered:

- **Input tokens** — the prompt sent to the model
- **Output tokens** — the model's response
- **Cache read tokens** — discounted rate for tokens served from provider cache
- **Cache write tokens** — slightly premium rate for tokens written to provider cache

On-device routing (local models) is always free — zero tokens billed, zero markup.

## Cost tracking

The dashboard shows:

- **Per-request cost** in the activity log
- **Daily cost** in the savings chart
- **Counterfactual comparison** — what you'd have paid without routing
- **Model distribution** — which models are consuming your budget
