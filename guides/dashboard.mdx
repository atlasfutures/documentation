The [Rayline dashboard](https://platform.rayline.ai) gives you visibility into how your routing is performing — cost savings, model distribution, and per-request detail.

## Accessing the dashboard

Sign in at [platform.rayline.ai](https://platform.rayline.ai). The main view is the **Router** page, which shows your usage analytics.

## KPI cards

At the top of the dashboard, you'll see headline metrics for the selected time range:

- **Total cost** — what you actually paid through Rayline
- **Requests** — total number of routed requests
- **Tokens** — total input + output tokens processed
- **Savings** — percentage saved compared to a counterfactual baseline

### Counterfactual baseline

The savings calculation compares your actual Rayline cost against what you'd have paid if every request used a single model at list price. You can toggle the baseline model:

- **Sonnet** (default) — compares against Claude Sonnet pricing
- **Opus** — compares against Claude Opus pricing
- **Haiku** — compares against Claude Haiku pricing

This helps you understand the value of routing relative to your likely alternative.

## Savings chart

The daily savings chart shows:
- **Rayline cost** (filled area) — what you paid each day
- **Baseline cost** (line) — what you'd have paid without routing

The gap between the two is your daily savings. Hover over any day to see exact numbers.

## Model distribution

The **Routing Breakdown** panel shows which models handled your traffic:

- Pie chart of request distribution by model
- The current routing strategy (ML or Rules)
- Percentage breakdown

The **Model Bars** section shows per-model usage over time — useful for seeing how Arc-1's model selection evolves as your workload changes.

## Activity log

Expand the **Recent Activity** section to see individual requests:

- Timestamp
- Model used
- Input/output tokens
- Cost
- Task classification

This is useful for debugging routing decisions — if a particular request routed to an unexpected model, you can see the classification that drove that decision.

## Time range

Toggle between time ranges to zoom in or out:

- **7 days** — recent activity
- **30 days** — default view
- **90 days** — quarterly overview
