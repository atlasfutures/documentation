**Rules Router's launch policy is 0% Rayline commission.** You pay for hosted model inference, with no additional Rules Router commission.

<Note>The zero-commission policy is the confirmed launch direction. The billing cutover has not been performed by this documentation change. Before launch, inspect the rate actually charged in your account; these docs do not retroactively change existing charges.</Note>

<span id="what-counts-as-usage" />
<span id="plans" />

## What you pay for

Input, cached input, output, and any other applicable provider token categories depend on the executed model and serving path. Use the model catalog and your actual usage records rather than an evergreen list of hardcoded model prices.

A local route uses your hardware. A hybrid session can still incur cloud inference costs. A Codex subscription-backed route follows the selected provider's terms; it is not the same bill as Rayline Platform routing.

## Not ARC Decisions

Rules Router's 0% commission is not a discount on ARC Decisions. [ARC Decisions](/arc/pricing) is billed per token it reads; see its current rates. [ARC Swift and Sage](/arc/pricing) have their own effective token rate cards. ARC Dynamic uses the served model's catalog inference price with no additional ARC routing fee.

<span id="why-it-still-saves-money" />

## Check an example

At the zero-commission launch rate, a hosted inference charge of $0.10 remains $0.10 before any applicable taxes or separately disclosed charges. No percentage routing markup is added.
