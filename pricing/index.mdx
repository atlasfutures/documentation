## How Rayline pricing works

**Model pass-through + 7.5% routing fee.**

You pay the model provider's per-token price, plus a 7.5% fee for Rayline's routing, classification, and infrastructure.

- **No monthly fee** — pay only for tokens consumed
- **No minimum spend** — start with `$5` or `$10`
- **On-device routing is free** — local models cost nothing

## Why it still saves money

The 7.5% fee is applied to a *much smaller base*. When Arc-1 routes a subagent call from Opus (`$15`/MTok output) to DeepSeek V4 Pro (`$3.48`/MTok output), you pay:

| | Without Rayline | With Rayline |
|---|---|---|
| Output price per 1M tokens | `$15.00` | `$3.48` × 1.075 = **`$3.74`** |
| **Savings** | — | **75%** |

The routing fee is 7.5% of the cheaper model's price — not 7.5% of the original. Net savings scale with how aggressively routing downgrades task-appropriate requests. See [The Frontier Tax](https://rayline.ai/research/the-frontier-tax) for the research behind this approach.

## Getting started

1. [Sign up](https://platform.rayline.ai/signup) for a Rayline account
2. Add funds (`$5`–`$50` to start, or any custom amount)
3. Optionally enable auto top-up to avoid running dry
4. [Set up your agent](/quickstart) and start routing
