## Zero balance / requests failing

If your requests are failing with balance-related errors:

1. Check your balance in the [dashboard](https://platform.rayline.ai)
2. Add funds — any amount from `$1` up
3. Enable **auto top-up** to prevent this in the future

### Enable auto top-up

In the dashboard, go to Setup/Account and configure:
- **Top-up amount**: How much to add (e.g., `$10`)
- **Threshold**: Recharge when balance drops below this (e.g., `$5`)

## Payment failed

If a top-up payment fails:

- **Card declined**: Check with your bank. Common causes: insufficient funds, international transaction blocks, card expiration.
- **Stripe error**: Try a different card or payment method.
- **Repeated failures**: Contact [help@rayline.ai](mailto:help@rayline.ai) with your account email.

## Unexpected charges

Rayline charges per token consumed, plus 7.5%. If your costs seem higher than expected:

1. **Check the activity log** in the dashboard — see which models handled your requests and at what token volume
2. **Check for long-running sessions** — a single complex task can generate many subagent calls
3. **Cache write tokens** are charged at a premium by some providers (notably Anthropic) — this is pass-through, not a Rayline surcharge
4. **Main agent requests** still cost frontier prices — savings come from subagent routing

### Cost per session

A rough guide:

| Session type | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Simple task, few subagents | `$0.10`–`$0.50` |
| Medium feature build | `$0.50`–`$3.00` |
| Complex refactor, many subagents | `$2.00`–`$10.00` |
| Heavy all-day usage | `$10.00`–`$30.00` |

## Refunds

Rayline doesn't issue refunds for consumed tokens — the cost is passed through to model providers. If you believe there's a billing error (e.g., duplicate charges, incorrect amounts), contact [help@rayline.ai](mailto:help@rayline.ai) with specifics.

## Enterprise billing

Enterprise customers have different billing arrangements (invoiced, committed-use). Contact your account manager for billing questions, or email [sales@rayline.ai](mailto:sales@rayline.ai).
