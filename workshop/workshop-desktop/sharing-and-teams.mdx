Workshop Desktop supports the same sharing and team features as Workshop Cloud — share conversations via link, publish apps with access control, and manage team billing and credits on a Scale or Max plan.

## Sharing Conversations

You can share any conversation via a private link — useful for showing someone a working prototype, demonstrating a concept, or getting support.

### How to Share

Sharing always applies to the conversation you currently have open, so open it first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    Open the project that contains the conversation you want to share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    In the **Conversations** panel, click the conversation. It becomes the current conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Click the project name with the caret (⌄) in the top-left of the window to expand the menu. The menu shows the current conversation name at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Share">
    Under the **CONVERSATION** section, click **Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Share Conversation Privately** dialog, click **Share**. Workshop creates a private link — anyone with the link can view the conversation, but it won't appear in search or public indexes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Click **Copy link and close** (or the copy icon next to the URL) and send the link to your audience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The link looks like `https://threads.workshop.ai/c/<conversation-id>` and renders your conversation in a clean, readable format so recipients experience it just as you did.

<Note>
  **Share** is disabled until the conversation has at least one message.
</Note>

### Before Sharing

- Check for accidentally included secrets, credentials, or API keys
- Confirm that any internal data is appropriate to share externally
- Open the link in a private browser window to verify it renders correctly

## Sharing Published Apps

When you publish an app from Workshop Desktop, you control who can access it:

- **Public** — Anyone with the URL. No sign-in required.
- **Private** — Only people you invite by email. Requires a paid plan.

If your project is linked to a **team subscription** (Scale or Max plan), you can use the **Team sharing** toggle in the Publish popover to grant access to all team members at once — no individual invitations needed.

For full details on publishing mechanics, see [Publishing](/workshop/workshop-desktop/publishing).

## Team Management

Teams let you collaborate with shared credit pools, centralized billing, and organization-wide settings.

<Info>
  Team management features require a **Scale** or **Max** plan subscription.
</Info>

### What Teams Include

- **Shared credit pool** — All team members draw from a common credit allocation
- **Centralized billing** — A single subscription covers all team members
- **Privacy controls** — Enforce privacy mode across all team members
- **Credit Pack bonuses** — 10% more credits on Scale; the largest discount on Max
- **Dedicated support** — Priority support through shared team channels

### Setting Up a Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upgrade to Scale or Max">
    Team features are available on the Scale and Max plans. Upgrade from your account settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure team settings">
    Set your team name, default member credit limits, and privacy enforcement preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite team members">
    Go to your team management dashboard, click **Invite Team Member**, and enter their email. You can optionally set individual credit limits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invited users receive an email with a link to join. Once they accept, they inherit team privacy settings and their credit limits take effect immediately.

### Member Roles

| Role | Capabilities |
|------|-------------|
| **Team Owner** | Full admin control: billing, invitations, member management, privacy enforcement, credit limit adjustments |
| **Team Member** | Access to shared credits, subject to team policies and individual credit limits |

### Seat Management

Both active team members and pending invitations count toward your seat limit. Free up capacity by removing a member, revoking a pending invitation, or upgrading your subscription.

<Note>
  Pending invitations reserve a seat until accepted, revoked, or expired (after 30 days).
</Note>

## Team Credit Management

- All team members draw from a **shared credit pool** set by the team owner's subscription
- Team owners can set **individual credit limits** per member (default: 1,000 credits)
- Members cannot exceed their individual limit even if team credits remain
- Credit Pack purchases receive a **10% bonus** for team accounts

Team owners have real-time visibility into team-wide and per-member credit usage from the team management dashboard.

## Privacy and Security

Team owners can **enforce privacy mode** for all members — ensuring conversations are never stored externally across your entire organization. Individual members cannot override this setting.
